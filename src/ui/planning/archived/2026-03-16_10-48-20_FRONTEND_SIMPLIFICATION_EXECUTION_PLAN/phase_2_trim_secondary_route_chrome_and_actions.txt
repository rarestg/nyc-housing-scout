-------

Core deliverables
-----------------
1. Remove summary strip usage from Posts, Review, and Debug.
2. Remove low-value route footers on Posts, Review, and Debug.
3. Keep only one action cluster per route.
4. Remove body-level duplicate action rows where the same actions already exist in the detail header.
5. Keep Posts evidence-first, Review triage-first, and Debug forensic.
6. Remove raw JSON and inspector clutter from Posts and Review unless it is clearly essential to the route's primary job.

Requirements
------------
- Do not strip Debug down so far that it loses the forensic handoff role.
- Do not move summary-strip removal onto Listings in this phase.
- Prefer deleting low-value copy over replacing it with new copy.
- Keep navigation between Listings, Posts, Review, and Debug intact.
